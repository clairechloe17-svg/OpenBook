The Problem

Every student learns differently.
Not every student gets that chance.

One teacher, thirty students, one pace. That's the reality in most middle school classrooms. OpenBook AI exists to close that gap — not to replace teachers, but to make sure every student gets the personalized support they deserve.

🏫

Teachers Are Outnumbered

The average middle school class has 25–30 students per teacher. There simply isn't enough time for a teacher to sit with every student who needs extra help — even when they want to.

🧠

Everyone Learns Differently

Some students get it from a diagram. Others need a story or a real-world example. Traditional classroom instruction can't always reach every learning style at once.

💸

Tutoring Isn't Affordable for Everyone

Private tutors cost $40–$100 per hour. That kind of personalized support should not be something only some families can afford. OpenBook AI makes it free and available to anyone.

🤝

AI as a Supplement, Not a Replacement

OpenBook AI doesn't replace teachers — it works alongside them. Think of it as a study partner who's always available, never impatient, and knows exactly how you learn best.

Why do we ask these questions?

OpenBook AI isn't a search engine — it's a tutor. And a great tutor pays attention to who you are, not just what you're asking.

  • ✓ Personalized explanations — your answers help the AI know whether to use diagrams, stories, formulas, or examples.
  • ✓ Adapted teaching styles — some students want short, sharp lessons. Others want to go deep. The AI adjusts.
  • ✓ Relatable examples — if you're into soccer or gaming, the AI can explain fractions using those things instead of abstract numbers.
  • ✓ Class alignment — if your teacher added a class code, the AI will focus on exactly what your class is working on.

🔒 Privacy first. Your information is only used to personalize your learning experience. It is never sold or shared.

For Teachers

A tool that works with you, not around you

OpenBook AI is designed to support what you're already doing in the classroom — with a human-in-the-loop teacher dashboard that keeps you in control.

🏷️

Create Class Groups

Generate a unique class code and share it with your students. They'll join your group and the AI will know the context of your specific class.

🎯

Guide the AI's Focus

Tell OpenBook what topics you're covering this week. Students — especially those still learning to prompt — will get help on exactly what's relevant to your curriculum.

📊

Track Student Progress

See which topics your students are struggling with most, so you can adjust your lessons and spend class time where it matters most.

🔗

Seamless Integration

Assign OpenBook as optional homework support, recommend it for students who need extra practice, or use it in-class for independent learning time.

👩‍🏫

Human-in-the-Loop Design

OpenBook AI is built around the idea that teachers are irreplaceable. The teacher dashboard keeps educators informed and in control — so AI fills the gaps without stepping on the expertise that only a human teacher can provide.
